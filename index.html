Scope & Basis

🎯 Scope

Clean and re-designate starboard storage tank WT4 S (347.1 m³) from VLSFO to LSMGO service. Cleaning is performed by a Fujairah workshop contractor under vessel and TSI supervision. The steam heating coils are retained and blanked internally. The common air/overflow system is not modified — cross-contamination is controlled operationally by ullage management. The only class-approved modification is the fuel transfer-line tie-in (drawing AD-E1-012-DL0402).

⚖️ Basis & Limitations

All quantities, durations and atmosphere values in this plan are indicative. Binding limits and procedures are those of the company SMS, the attending marine chemist, Bureau Veritas, and the bunker/charter documents. Critical values are to be confirmed against these sources before and during execution.

3

Key Challenges

In order of priority — the four issues that govern the whole conversion.

Priority 1

🧽 Cleaning to a distillate standard

Remove all VLSFO clingage and sludge — including around the retained heating coils — so the tank meets LSMGO specification, not merely gas-free.

Priority 2

🔧 Steam coil isolation & internal blanking

Coils are retained; supply/return must be isolated and the penetrations blanked internally, then documented (any future work is a fresh enclosed-space entry).

Priority 3

📟 Level alarm & gauging re-calibration

The density change (HFO 0.960 → LSMGO ~0.86) requires the level transmitter and high-level/overfill alarm to be re-ranged and function-tested before the tank enters service.

Priority 4

🌀 Overflow / common vent

No hardware modification; no non-return device exists on the gravity overflow line. Controlled operationally — WT4 S kept below overflow level, header kept free-draining.

PHASE 2Steam Heating Coils (retained)▶
  1. Isolate steam. Isolate the steam supply/return to WT4 S coils at the manifold; confirm this does not interrupt heating to other tanks on the same steam main.C/E
  2. Internal blanking. Blank the coil penetrations from inside the tank.Contractor (supervised)
  3. Clean the coils externally. Coils remain in place; wash and wipe them clean of VLSFO clingage along their length and brackets to remove a residual-contamination source.Contractor
  4. Document the blanks. Photograph and record the coil blanks (as-fitted). Any future work on a blanked coil is a fresh enclosed-space entry and must be traceable.TSI

PHASE 6Overflow / Common Vent Control (no modification)▶

PHASE 7Level Alarm & Gauging Re-calibration (mandatory)▶
  1. Confirm transmitter type. Confirm the level-transmitter type for WT4 S (LT FC-25 / LAH FC-27).C/E / electrician
  2. Re-range for new density. Re-range / re-calibrate for the density change (HFO 0.960 → LSMGO ~0.86, ≈10%). • Pressure / hydrostatic type — re-calibration is mandatory; otherwise the tank reads ~10% low and the high-level alarm trips late, creating an overfill risk. • Radar / float type — the level reading is unaffected by density, but the high-level / overflow alarm setpoint must still be verified. C/E / electrician
  3. Function-test the alarm. Function-test the high-level alarm at the corrected setpoint.C/E
HOLD

Overfill protection (SOLAS) confirmed before the tank enters service.
