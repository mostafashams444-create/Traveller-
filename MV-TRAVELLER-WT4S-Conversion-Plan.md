## 1. Scope

Clean and re-designate starboard storage tank **WT4 S (347.1 m³)** from VLSFO to LSMGO service.
Tank cleaning is performed by a Fujairah workshop contractor under vessel and TSI supervision.
The steam heating coils are retained and blanked internally. The common air/overflow system is not
modified — cross-contamination is controlled operationally by ullage management. The only
class-approved modification is the fuel transfer-line tie-in (drawing AD-E1-012-DL0402).

## 2. Basis & Limitations

All quantities, durations and atmosphere values in this plan are indicative. Binding limits and
procedures are those of the company SMS, the attending marine chemist, Bureau Veritas, and the
bunker/charter documents. Critical values are to be confirmed against these sources before and
during execution.

## 3. Key Challenges (in order of priority)

1. **Cleaning to a distillate standard** — Removing all VLSFO clingage and sludge — including
   around the retained heating coils — so the tank meets LSMGO specification, not merely gas-free.
2. **Steam coil isolation & internal blanking** — Coils are retained; supply/return must be
   isolated and the penetrations blanked internally, then documented (any future work is a fresh
   enclosed-space entry).
3. **Level alarm & gauging re-calibration** — The density change (HFO 0.960 → LSMGO ~0.86)
   requires the level transmitter and high-level/overfill alarm to be re-ranged and function-tested
   before the tank enters service.
4. **Overflow / common vent** — No hardware modification; no non-return device exists on the
   gravity overflow line. Controlled operationally — WT4 S kept below overflow level, header kept
   free-draining.

## PHASE 0 — Approvals & Mobilisation (pre-arrival)

1. **Commercial confirmation.** Owners/Charterers confirm WT4 S selection and the capacity
   shift (VLSFO 838→491; LSMGO 176→523 m³) and accept the reduced VLSFO endurance.
   *(Management / Owners)*
2. **Pre-authorisation.** Obtain written approval for the Fujairah contractor, marine chemist and
   BV attendance, with cost, before any commitment. *(TSI → Management)*
3. **Class drawing.** Prepare the WT4 S transfer-line tie-in drawing (basis DL0402) and submit to
   Bureau Veritas for approval. *(TSI / Engineering)*

> **HOLD POINT** — No hot work until the modification drawing is approved.

4. **Mobilisation.** Arrange contractor, marine chemist, slop reception and BV surveyor at
   Fujairah. *(Agent / TSI)*

## PHASE 1 — Arrival & Tank Preparation

5. **Empty the tank.** Transfer remaining VLSFO out of WT4 S to nominated tank(s); confirm
   receiving-tank ullage with the C/E. Strip to lowest suction. *(C/E / crew)*
6. **Isolate the tank.** Positively isolate WT4 S from the fuel system: close and lash valves, blank
   the fuel suction/fill connection; confirm no fuel can enter the tank during the works. *(C/E)*
7. **Record.** Log all internal transfers and stripping in the Oil Record Book Part I. *(C/E)*

## PHASE 2 — Steam Heating Coils (retained)

8. **Isolate steam.** Isolate the steam supply/return to WT4 S coils at the manifold; confirm this
   does not interrupt heating to other tanks on the same steam main. *(C/E)*
9. **Internal blanking.** Blank the coil penetrations from inside the tank. *(Contractor (supervised))*
10. **Clean the coils externally.** Coils remain in place; wash and wipe them clean of VLSFO
    clingage along their length and brackets to remove a residual-contamination source. *(Contractor)*
11. **Document the blanks.** Photograph and record the coil blanks (as-fitted). Any future work on
    a blanked coil is a fresh enclosed-space entry and must be traceable. *(TSI)*

> **HOLD POINT** — Coil blanking arrangement photographed and recorded before close-up.

## PHASE 3 — Tank Cleaning by Workshop (supervise & accept)

12. **Define acceptance standard.** Issue the cleaning acceptance standard to the contractor in
    writing before work starts (visual cleanliness, no oily film, no free residue around coils and
    structure). *(TSI)*
13. **Mucking out.** Remove heavy sludge and sediment to slop drums. *(Contractor)*
14. **Hot wash.** Hot-water wash with alkaline degreaser to a distillate-clean standard, not merely
    gas-free. *(Contractor)*
15. **Strip slops.** Strip slops continuously during washing. *(Contractor)*
16. **Slop disposal.** Dispose of all slops and sludge to an approved Fujairah shore reception
    facility; retain receipts; record in ORB Part I. *(Contractor / C/E)*

## PHASE 4 — Gas-Freeing, Entry & Acceptance

17. **Ventilate.** Continuous forced ventilation until gas-free. *(Contractor / C/E)*
18. **Atmosphere testing.** Multi-gas testing (oxygen plus toxic and flammable gases) per IMO
    Res. MSC.581(110), in force 03 Dec 2025. *(Marine chemist)*

> **HOLD POINT** — Marine chemist issues the gas-free certificate before any entry.

19. **Enclosed-space entry.** Entry under permit: standby attendant, continuous communication,
    SCBA/rescue gear ready, ATEX lighting, continuous ventilation with periodic re-testing logged.
    *(C/E / crew)*
20. **Final clean & inspection.** Wipe, scrape and rinse residual spots; inspect to the
    distillate-clean standard. *(Contractor (supervised))*
21. **Acceptance.** Accept the tank as distillate-clean, with photo record, before closing up.
    *(TSI / C/E)*

> **HOLD POINT** — Tank accepted as distillate-clean (with photo record) before close-up.

## PHASE 5 — Transfer-Line Tie-in (only class-approved modification)

22. **Prepare for hot work.** Re-confirm gas-free and isolation immediately before; post fire watch.
    *(C/E)*
23. **Execute the tie-in.** Connect WT4 S (MDO TK(S)) to the MDO transfer system per the
    BV-approved drawing; flow meter and pumps per P&ID (DL0402). *(Contractor)*
24. **Pressure / tightness test.** Pressure-test the new line for tightness. *(Contractor / C/E)*
25. **Class verification.** BV surveyor verifies the completed tie-in and tightness test.
    *(Bureau Veritas)*

> **HOLD POINT** — BV verification of the tie-in obtained.

## PHASE 6 — Overflow / Common Vent Control (no modification)

26. **Confirm line-up.** Confirm the existing line-up of the common air/overflow header (DL0403);
    confirm valve integrity and that the header is free-draining toward the overflow tank. *(C/E)*
27. **Set ullage control.** Establish WT4 S maximum working level below the overflow level at all
    times so LSMGO cannot reach the shared header. *(C/E / Master)*
28. **Adjacent-tank rule.** Adjacent HFO tanks on the same header are not filled above their
    overflow level during LSMGO operations, to preclude back-flow surcharge. *(C/E)*
29. **Document the control.** Record ullage management as a formal operational control; note
    there is no non-return device on the overflow line (control is operational by design). *(TSI)*

## PHASE 7 — Level Alarm & Gauging Re-calibration (mandatory)

30. **Confirm transmitter type.** Confirm the level-transmitter type for WT4 S
    (LT FC-25 / LAH FC-27). *(C/E / electrician)*
31. **Re-range for new density.** Re-range / re-calibrate for the density change
    (HFO 0.960 → LSMGO ~0.86, ≈10%). See sub-cases below. *(C/E / electrician)*
    - *Pressure / hydrostatic type* — re-calibration is mandatory; otherwise the tank reads ~10% low
      and the high-level alarm trips late, creating an overfill risk.
    - *Radar / float type* — the level reading is unaffected by density, but the high-level / overflow
      alarm setpoint must still be verified.
32. **Function-test the alarm.** Function-test the high-level alarm at the corrected setpoint. *(C/E)*

> **HOLD POINT** — Overfill protection (SOLAS) confirmed before the tank enters service.

33. **Update loading data.** Sounding/ullage tables are unchanged (geometry); update only the
    density/mass entry in the loading computer (0.96 → ~0.86). *(C/E)*

## PHASE 8 — Commissioning & First LSMGO

34. **Flush the line.** Flush the new transfer line; confirm valve line-up to LSMGO. *(C/E)*
35. **Load first parcel.** Load a small first parcel of LSMGO into WT4 S; draw a representative
    sample. *(C/E)*
36. **Lab test.** Test the sample against ISO 8217:2024 distillate (0.10% S ECA, flash ≥60 °C,
    viscosity/sediment within limits; final limits per charter/bunker contract). *(C/E / TSI)*

> **HOLD POINT** — Sample confirmed on specification before declaring the tank in service.

## PHASE 9 — Documentation, PMS & Close-out

37. **Update capacities.** Update the capacity plan, sounding tables and loading manual / loading
    computer (WT4 S as LSMGO). *(TSI / C/E)*
38. **Update markings.** Update tank markings (hull and engine room). *(C/E)*
39. **Update PMS.** Update AMOS — service, sampling and inspection tasks for the new LSMGO
    tank; add coil-blank and alarm-calibration records. *(C/E / TSI)*
40. **File records.** File the BV record, gas-free certificate, slop receipts, as-fitted tie-in/coil-blank
    drawing, and the first-parcel lab report. *(TSI)*
41. **Close out.** Complete the task risk assessment / JSA close-out and lessons learned. *(TSI / C/E)*
